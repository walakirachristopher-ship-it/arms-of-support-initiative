🌍 Who We Are

Arms Of Support Initiative is a Ugandan organisation dedicated to empowering vulnerable children through education, health, and community support.

👁️ Our Vision

A Uganda where every child has access to education, health, and a safe home.

🎯 Our Mission

To rescue, nurture, and educate vulnerable children through community-based programs and international partnership.

💎 Our Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Compassion
  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Excellence

🇺🇬🇪🇺 Our Partnership

We are proudly supported by sponsors from the Netherlands, Belgium, and other European countries who share our vision of hope and opportunity.
