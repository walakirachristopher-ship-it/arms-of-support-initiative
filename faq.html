❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions.

How can I sponsor a child?

Contact us through our contact page and we will guide you through the process.

Where does my money go?

100% of sponsor donations go directly to supporting children's education, health, and well-being.

Can I visit the children I sponsor?

Yes! We welcome sponsors to visit and see the impact of their support firsthand.

How do I get updates?

We provide regular updates, photos, and progress reports for all sponsored children.
